Privacy Policy

The short version: what you do in my apps stays on your device. I don't collect it, sell it or share it.

Effective October 3, 2026 · Covers all apps published by Alex Slem on the App Store

What I collect

Nothing. My apps don't have accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and they don't send your information to me or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Some apps save things so they're there next time: journal entries and reflections, breathing sessions, heart-rate readings, game progress, and room scans. All of it is stored only on your iPhone or iPad. If you use iCloud backup, Apple may include it in your device backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

iCloud and sharing (Run It)

Run It saves your pieces, songs, run videos, frames, notes and corrections in your own private iCloud using Apple's CloudKit, so they sync across your devices. I can't see any of it. If you share a piece, only the people you invite can see it, and you choose whether they can make changes. You can remove someone or stop sharing at any time. Run It analyzes music and video on your device and never sends them to me or to any server of mine.

Permissions

An app asks before it uses any of these, and only for the feature that needs it:

  • Health and heart rate: to show and log your heart rate during a session.
  • Camera and motion sensors: for live scanning and visual features. The camera feed is processed on the device and isn't uploaded.
  • Microphone: only where an app records or reacts to sound, and only while you're using it.
  • Files and media library: to play your own music and videos.
  • Local Network and Bluetooth: to look for devices on your own network or nearby.

You can turn any permission off at any time in Settings.

Sharing and selling

I don't sell, rent or share your information, because I don't have it.

Children

My apps don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its data is deleted from your device.

For Run It, data you keep in iCloud stays there until you delete it in the app. Deleting a piece removes it from your iCloud, and from everyone you shared it with.

Changes

If an app ever changes how it handles data, I'll update this page and the date above before that update ships.
