About

Built by hand, with a heartbeat.

I'm Alex Slem. I worked construction most of my life, moved into phone repair in 2015, and that led me into running operations. I wrote my first line of code in April 2026, and I haven't stopped since.

Every app here has a heartbeat in it. I call it living, breathing code.

Most of my apps are about grounding: breath, stillness, a quiet place to land. The bigger work is Bridget and LunalithCore, a brain for AI with memory and boundaries, and Groundline, which tests whether AI keeps people grounded. I want people and AI to be safe with each other, especially the people who are most vulnerable.
