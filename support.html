Common questions

A feature isn't working, like heart rate, the camera or a scan. Most features need a permission. Open Settings, scroll to the app, and turn on what it asks for (Health, Camera, Microphone, Local Network or Bluetooth). Then reopen the app.

Where is my data? On your device. My apps don't have accounts or cloud servers, so your journal entries, sessions and readings stay on your iPhone or iPad.

I deleted the app. Can I get my data back? Usually not. Because the data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes it. Back up your device before deleting an app if you want to keep anything.

Do the apps cost anything? No. They're free to download.

Are the wellness apps medical advice? No. Breathwork, heart-rate and meditation features are for relaxation and reflection, not diagnosis or treatment. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988.
